Systems / Windows · own repo

BiggerBrother — a traffic observatory for your own machine

Ties every DNS query and socket on Windows to the process that made it, via ETW — plus an in-browser request recorder that shows exactly which cookies your browser handed to which trackers. Local-only: no cloud, no TLS interception, nothing leaves the machine.

  • Per-process attribution: who talked to whom, and who owns the far end
  • In-page cookie/tracker capture without touching TLS
  • Per-process kill switch — drops an app's live TCP connections with Cut or Choke
